Why Home Screen Blueprint works

Most people fail because they start randomly—renaming folders, moving icons, then giving up. This app gives you a structure first, so your home screen stays clean even as your usage changes.

You’ll avoid common mistakes like:

  • Mixing “daily” apps with “rarely used” apps
  • Creating too many pages too early
  • Over-organizing and making it harder to find things

Three organization styles (Plans)

1) Function

Group apps by what they do.

Example folders: Work / Life / Entertainment / Tools

Best for: people who search apps by purpose.

2) Context

Group apps by when/where you use them.

Example folders: Morning / Day / Night / On the Go

Best for: people whose app usage changes by time or situation.

3) Frequency

Group apps by how often you use them.

Example folders: Daily / Sometimes / Rarely

Best for: people who want a cleaner first page fast.

What you get

  • Recommended folder blueprint

How it works (about 10 minutes)

  1. Pick a plan (Function / Context / Frequency)
  2. Review the blueprint (folder set + naming guidance)
  3. Follow the guide steps (move low-frequency apps, build folders, tidy pages)
  4. Finish with a stable home screen you can maintain

Tip: Don’t try to perfect everything on day one. Start with a stable structure, then adjust gradually.

FAQ

Does the app reorganize my home screen automatically?

No. iOS doesn’t allow apps to fully rearrange your icons automatically. Home Screen Blueprint provides a plan and checklist so you can do it quickly and correctly.

Do I need to log in?

No login required.

Is there any data collection?

The app is designed to work without collecting personal data.
